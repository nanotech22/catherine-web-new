About Catherine

A life built around creative connection.

Catherine Reid is a composer, music director, arranger, orchestrator, pianist, teacher, gardener, and painter. After retiring from Lake George High School, she has focused more of her time on creating music and musical events.

Much of her work explores the relationship between music and visual or spoken art: rhythm and pattern, harmony and color, musical texture, the arc of dance, and the story or emotional atmosphere inside a poem or painting.

Her favorite way to create is collaboratively—with writers, singers, composers, musicians, artists, and performers.
